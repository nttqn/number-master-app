Support for Number Master

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Number Master is a runner game for Android: swipe between five lanes, pass through math gates to grow your number, and break through the walls at the end of each level.

How to play

  • Swipe left or right to change lanes.
  • Blue gates add or multiply your number; red gates subtract or divide it.
  • Loose numbers smaller than yours are absorbed; touching a bigger one ends the run.
  • Avoid saws, time your way past sliding saws and changing gates, and don't fall through holes in the bridge.
  • Break every wall at the end of the level, then climb the bonus staircase (x2 to x10) for more coins.
  • Spend coins in the Shop on upgrades, skins and trails.
  • Tap the pause button (or the device back button) to pause mid-run.

Common questions

The leaderboard says "not available"

The leaderboards use Google Play Games Services. Make sure you're signed in to a Google account on your device and have an internet connection, then try again.

I don't hear any sound or music

Check the Sound and Music toggles on the main menu or the pause screen, and make sure your device isn't in silent mode.

Something's broken, or you have feedback

Please include your device model and Android version (e.g. "Pixel 8, Android 15") along with a description of what happened — it helps a lot in tracking issues down.

Privacy Policy for Number Master

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Number Master ("the app") is a casual runner game for Android. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information collected directly by the app

Number Master does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. The only data the app stores itself is kept locally on your device (via Android's standard app storage) and is never transmitted anywhere by the app:

  • Your progress: the highest level unlocked and your best number per level.
  • Your coins, shop upgrades, and the skins and trails you own and use.
  • Your sound and music on/off preferences.

This local data is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

The app uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in with your Google account and to display and submit leaderboard scores (the highest level you've cleared and the biggest number you've reached). Sign-in happens through Google's own systems — Number Master never sees or stores your Google account credentials. Your Google Play Games profile (display name, avatar, and player ID) and submitted scores are handled according to Google's Privacy Policy. Playing the game does not require signing in to Play Games.

Advertising

Number Master shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the game free: a banner during play, an occasional full-screen ad after a game over, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to double the coins from a level. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads, and may use this data to personalize ads depending on your device and account settings. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Number Master directly. You can review or adjust how Google uses this data for ads through your device's ad settings, and learn more at Google's Ads Privacy & Terms.

Data sharing

Number Master does not sell your data, and does not share any data with third parties beyond the Google services (Play Games Services, AdMob) described above, which are necessary for the leaderboard and advertising features to function.

Children's privacy

Number Master is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and Play Games sign-in are provided by Google's SDKs, which offer their own settings and controls for compliance with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. Check back here for the latest version.

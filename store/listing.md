## English (en-US)

**Title**

Number Master: Math Runner

**Short description**

Pick the best gates, grow your number and smash every wall in your way!

**Full description**

Run, do the math, and grow your number as big as you can!

Number Master is a fast, bright runner on a bridge high above the clouds. Swipe between five lanes, pass through the gates that make you bigger and dodge the ones that shrink you. At the end of every level, a row of walls is waiting. Only a big enough number breaks through.

SIMPLE TO PLAY
• Swipe left and right to change lanes. That's all.
• Blue gates add and multiply, red gates subtract and divide.
• Grab loose numbers smaller than yours to grow. Touch a bigger one and the run is over.
• Avoid the spinning saws.

NEW CHALLENGES AS YOU GO
• Sliding saws that sweep across the best gates. Time your pass!
• Gates that keep changing between good and bad. Hit them at the right moment.
• Holes in the bridge. Don't fall through!
• Every level is a little faster and a little trickier.

BREAK THE WALLS, CLIMB THE STAIRS
• Smash through every wall at the end of the level.
• Whatever number is left climbs the bonus staircase from x2 up to x10.
• The higher you climb, the more coins you earn.

UPGRADES AND STYLE
• Start Number: begin every run bigger.
• Income: earn more coins every level.
• Six player skins: Cube, Ball, Candy, Wood, Slime and Gem.
• Trails behind you: Sparkle, Rainbow and Fire.

COMPETE
• Google Play Games leaderboards for the highest level and the biggest number.

Bright music, punchy effects, and no waiting: just run, merge and break through.

Free to play, with ads.

Chạy, tính nhanh và nuôi con số của bạn lớn nhất có thể!

Number Master là game chạy vô tận tươi sáng trên cây cầu giữa trời mây. Vuốt qua lại giữa năm làn, đi qua các cổng làm bạn lớn lên và né các cổng làm bạn nhỏ đi. Cuối mỗi màn là một dãy tường chắn đường. Chỉ con số đủ lớn mới phá được.

PHÁ TƯỜNG, LEO CẦU THANG
